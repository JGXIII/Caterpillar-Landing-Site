1. Who we are

Caterpillar Calorie Tracker ("Caterpillar", "the app", "we") is an iOS application for tracking food, hydration and related wellbeing habits, operated from Australia. By downloading or using the app you agree to these terms.

2. What Caterpillar is and isn't

Caterpillar provides estimates of calories, macronutrients, portion sizes and hydration, generated from food databases, barcode data, user input and AI analysis of photos. Estimates can be wrong. The app is a general-wellbeing tool, not a medical device, and nothing in it constitutes medical, dietetic or other professional health advice. Consult a qualified professional before making significant dietary changes, particularly if you are pregnant, have a medical condition, or have a history of disordered eating. Caterpillar is not intended for users under 16.

3. Your account

  • Caterpillar uses an account so that your history, streaks and achievements are tied to you rather than to a single device, and survive changing or losing a phone. You can create one with Sign in with Apple, with Google, or with an email address and password.
  • You are responsible for the security of whichever credentials you use. We never see your Apple or Google password. If you use an email password, it is stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider.
  • You must be 16 or older to create an account.
  • You may delete your account in-app at any time (Nest → Delete Account). Deletion is processed within 48 hours and permanently removes your cloud data. You can export your data to CSV first.

Pricing, billing, renewal and cancellation are handled by Apple under their terms, and subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in your Apple account settings. Features included in Pro are described at the point of purchase and may change over time. If Pro lapses, Pro-only data services may stop and associated cloud content may be scheduled for deletion after notice.

5. Fair use of AI features

AI scanning and AI search run on a shared, metered service that costs us money per request. Usage is therefore subject to per-device limits on the number of requests per hour and per day. These limits apply to all users including Pro subscribers, are set well above ordinary personal use, and may be adjusted. Attempting to circumvent them (for example by automating requests or resetting counters) is a breach of these terms.

6. Acceptable use

Don't attempt to disrupt the service, probe or bypass its security, scrape or bulk-extract its databases, reverse engineer the app except as the law permits, or submit unlawful or deliberately false content through community features such as food submissions.

7. Community food data

When you submit or correct food data, including nutrition-label scans that are verified and added to Caterpillar's shared verified-food database, you grant us a perpetual, royalty-free licence to use, edit and distribute that contribution within the service. It may, after review, be shown to other users. Photographs you attach as evidence are stored with the submission and reviewed by a human, so submissions must not contain personal information, and you should not include anything in frame you would not want a moderator to see. Approved contributions remain in the shared database if you later delete your account, because other users rely on them; they contain product facts, not personal information.

8. Importing your data from other apps

Caterpillar can import history you export from another tracker. You are responsible for having the right to use that data. Imported entries are treated as your own logs: they fill gaps in your history and never overwrite days you've already recorded, and they don't earn achievements or streaks, which are reserved for logging done in Caterpillar.
