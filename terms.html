1. Who we are

2. What Caterpillar is and isn't

Caterpillar provides estimates of calories, macronutrients, portion sizes and hydration, generated from food databases, barcode data, your own input and AI analysis of photos and descriptions. Estimates can be wrong. The app is a general wellbeing tool, not a medical device, and nothing in it is medical, dietetic or other professional health advice. Talk to a qualified professional before making significant changes to your diet, particularly if you are pregnant, have a medical condition, or have a history of disordered eating. Caterpillar is not intended for anyone under 16.

7. Community food data

When you submit or correct food data, including nutrition label scans that are verified and added to Caterpillar's shared verified food database, you grant us a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free licence to use, edit and distribute that contribution within the service, and it may be shown to other users. Photographs you attach as evidence are stored with the submission and may be reviewed by a person, so they must not contain personal information or anything you would not want a moderator to see. Approved contributions stay in the shared database if you later delete your account, because other users rely on them; they contain product facts, not personal information.

8. Importing your data from other apps

Caterpillar can import history you export from another tracker. You are responsible for having the right to use that data. Imported entries are treated as your own logs: they fill gaps in your history and never overwrite days you have already recorded, and they don't earn achievements or streaks, which are reserved for logging done in Caterpillar.
