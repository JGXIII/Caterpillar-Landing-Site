Log a meal in seconds, not minutes

Four ways in, zero friction. Pick whichever suits the moment — Caterpillar remembers your portions and habits either way.

📸

AI Meal Scan

Photograph your plate and Caterpillar identifies every component. On Pro-model iPhones, LiDAR depth measures your actual portion size — not a guess from a stock photo. Packaged products are read straight off the label for accuracy.

🏷️

Instant barcode scans

Point, beep, logged — with excellent coverage of Woolies, Coles and Aldi products. Scan five things in a row with Log & Scan Next. Every scan becomes searchable forever after.

🔎

Search that gets you

Australian food data first (AUSNUT & FSANZ), plus global databases. Type "maccas" and it knows what you mean. Nothing found? Ask AI and it will track the food down for you.

📋

Menu Scan

Eating out? Photograph the restaurant menu and pick what you ordered — Caterpillar estimates the dishes so a pub lunch doesn't derail your day.

Progress you can actually see

Most trackers give you a number. Caterpillar gives you a world.

🐛

Your caterpillar companion

It munches when you log, sips when you hydrate, and grows along your day. Overeat and it gets a little dizzy — honest feedback, zero shame.

🦋

The Butterfly Gallery

Every healthy habit earns puzzle pieces that reveal collectible butterfly artwork — including secret achievements you'll only discover by living well. Your collection is saved to your account, safe across devices and reinstalls.

💧

Hydration, done properly

A dew drop fills with every drink — and it knows coffee isn't water. Customise your quick-drinks list, or create your own (yes, your electrolyte mix can count at 110%).

📈

Your whole journey

Weight trends, streaks, weekly insights and Apple Health sync. Sign in with Apple and your history follows you — delete the app, come back next year, it's all still there.
