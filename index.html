Log a meal in seconds, not minutes

Five ways in, all of them fast. Pick whichever suits the moment. Caterpillar remembers your portions and habits either way.

Snap Your Plate

Photograph your plate and Caterpillar identifies every component. On Pro-model iPhones, LiDAR depth measures your actual portion, not a guess from a stock photo. Tell it "130 g pasta" and it takes your word for it.

Instant barcode scans

Point, beep, logged, with strong coverage of Woolies, Coles and Aldi. Scan five things in a row with Log & Scan Next. Every scan becomes searchable forever after.

Menu Scan

Eating out? Photograph the menu and tap what you ordered. Portion each dish up or down so a pub lunch doesn't derail the day.

Just say it

Talk your meal in while your hands are busy. Or type it. Australian food data comes first, so "maccas" and "Weet-Bix" land where you expect.

Favourites that stick

The things you eat every day are one tap from logged, at the portion you actually have, mixed drinks and powders included.

The Butterfly Gallery

Healthy habits earn puzzle pieces that uncover collectible butterfly artwork, including secrets you'll only find by living well.

A tracker that keeps you honest

Most apps quietly round in your favour, because a happy number keeps you opening the app. Caterpillar would rather be right.

Cooked, not dry

Pasta, rice and oats roughly triple in weight when you cook them. Log "130 g pasta" over a plate of dinner and Caterpillar counts it as cooked, and says so on the entry.

Hydration that doesn't flatter you

A dew drop fills with everything you drink, coloured by what it actually was. Coffee and tea count properly: the research says they hydrate you about as well as water. Spirits don't: a neat nip takes water back out of the drop, and you can see it happen.

Australian food, first

Built on FSANZ food composition data, because Australian mince really is leaner than the American figures most apps quietly use. Barcodes, nutrition panels and a shared verified-food database that gets better every time somebody scans something new.

Progress you can actually see

Most trackers give you a number. Caterpillar gives you a day you can read at a glance.

Grows

Your caterpillar lengthens as the day fills, munches when you log and sips when you drink.

Remembers

Sign in and your history, streaks and gallery follow you: new phone, reinstall, next year.

No ads

Nothing is sold, nothing is rented, and nothing follows you around the internet.

Yours
