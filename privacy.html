Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 August 2026

The short version. No ads, no selling data, no tracking you around the internet. We collect the minimum needed to make the app work, most of it stays on your phone, you can export all of it whenever you like, and you can delete everything from inside the app.

1. Data that stays on your device

  • Your daily logs, history, streaks, achievements, favourites, custom foods, hydration entries and settings live on your iPhone.
  • Meal-scan photos are kept as small compressed copies on-device for your timeline.
  • Apple Health data (steps, energy, exercise, sleep, weight, water) is read and written only with your permission, on-device, via HealthKit. Note that your day summaries (which can include step and energy totals, and the hours of sleep shown in a daily check-in) form part of your cloud backup if you are signed in.

2. Data backed up to your account

We back up your tracking data so it survives reinstalls and device changes: day history, achievements and gallery progress, streaks, hydration entries, favourites and custom foods, weight entries (numbers and notes, not progress photos), daily check-ins (hunger, sleep and notes), any macro targets you entered from a coach, your spreadsheet column layout, your drink list, learned food preferences, your profile details from onboarding, and app settings. This is stored with our backend provider (Supabase) and protected by row-level security, so only your authenticated account can read or write your rows.

3. Signing in

Caterpillar uses an account so your history survives losing or changing a phone. You can create one three ways:

  • Sign in with Apple. We receive an anonymous Apple identifier and, on first sign-in only, the name and email you approve, which may be Apple's private relay address. We never see your Apple ID password.
  • Sign in with Google. We receive your Google account identifier and email address. We never see your Google password.
  • Email and password. Your email address is stored, and your password is handled and hashed by our authentication provider. It is never stored by us in a readable form and never leaves your device in the clear. Password resets are sent to that address.

4. AI photo and text analysis

When you use Snap Your Plate, Menu Scan or a nutrition-label scan, your photo (compressed) is sent over an encrypted connection through our server function to our AI provider (Google's Gemini API) solely to generate the nutrition analysis. Optional numeric portion measurements derived on-device from LiDAR travel with it, never raw depth data. The result is returned to your device. If you use "Ask AI" or voice input, the text or audio of what you asked is processed the same way. We do not build advertising profiles from any of it.

Requests are rate-limited per device to keep the shared service usable. Enforcing that involves counting requests on your device; the counts are not tied to your identity.

5. Food search

Search queries are sent to food databases (our own curated database, plus Open Food Facts, USDA and Australian government datasets via our server) to return results. Queries are used to answer the search and are not linked to your account.

6. Community and quality data

  • Food submissions and corrections you choose to make are sent to us for review and may be shared with other users once approved. The photos you took as evidence are sent and stored with the submission, up to three of them (nutrition panel, barcode, front of pack), so a human can check the values against the actual packaging. Don't include anything in frame you wouldn't want a moderator to see.
  • When you scan a nutrition label and the reading is verified against the product, the product's name, brand and nutrition values are added to Caterpillar's shared verified-food database so other users benefit, together with those same evidence photos. Only product facts are shared, never anything identifying you.
  • The app sends anonymous, device-scoped reliability signals (a random install identifier and, for example, "this device received this food update") so we can tell whether features actually work. These are not tied to your identity or account.

7. Anonymous usage analytics

Caterpillar records which features are used (that a meal was logged, that a paywall was shown, that onboarding finished) so we can tell what is working and what isn't. This is deliberately built so it cannot identify you:

  • Events are keyed to a random identifier generated once per install. It is not your account id, email, Apple ID or device id, and it is never sent anywhere else. Deleting the app ends that identity permanently.
  • No content is ever recorded: not a search query, not a food name, not a weight, not a photo, not a note. Only small facts, such as which scan type a meal came from, which settings are switched on, and the 1 to 10 hunger and sleep ratings from the optional daily check-in.
  • You can turn it off in the app, and nothing further is sent.

8. Subscriptions

If you buy Caterpillar Pro, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple's in-app purchase system. We never see your payment details. The app checks your entitlement with Apple to know whether Pro is active.

9. Importing and exporting

If you import history from another tracker, you pick the file yourself and it is read entirely on your device. We don't browse your files and the file isn't uploaded anywhere. The meals, weigh-ins, water and activity it contains then become part of your own history, and your cloud backup if you're signed in, exactly like data you logged in Caterpillar.

You can export everything you've logged as CSV files at any time, straight from the app.

10. Notifications

Notifications are optional and off until you allow them. Reminders are scheduled and delivered on your device. Their content, meaning what you have or haven't logged, is never sent to a server to generate them.

11. What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad SDKs, no third-party analytics or attribution trackers.
  • No selling or renting personal information, ever.
  • No reading your Apple Health data off your device beyond the day summaries described in section 1.

12. Retention and deletion

Cloud data is kept while your account exists. Delete your account in-app (Nest → Delete Account) and your request is actioned within 48 hours, permanently removing your cloud data. Deleting the app removes all local data immediately. Contributions already approved into the shared food database (which contain product facts, not personal information) remain, as other users rely on them. You can also email us to exercise access or correction rights under the Australian Privacy Principles.

13. Children

Caterpillar is not directed at children and is not intended for users under 16.

14. Changes

If this policy changes materially we'll say so in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.
