Privacy Policy

Effective 20 July 2026

Short version: no ads, no selling data, no tracking you around the internet. We collect the minimum needed to make the app work, most of it stays on your phone, and you can delete everything from inside the app.

1. Data that stays on your device

  • Your daily logs, history, streaks, achievements, favourites, custom foods, hydration entries and settings live on your iPhone.
  • Meal-scan photos are kept as small compressed copies on-device for your timeline (cloud backup of photos is a planned Pro feature).
  • Apple Health data (steps, energy, exercise, weight, water) is read and written only with your permission, on-device, via HealthKit. Note: your day summaries — which can include step and energy totals — are part of your cloud backup if you sign in.

2. Data backed up to your account

We back up your tracking data so it survives reinstalls and device changes: day history, achievements and gallery progress, streaks, hydration entries, favourites and custom foods, weight entries (numbers and notes — not progress photos), your drink list, learned food preferences, and app settings. This is stored with our backend provider (Supabase) and protected by row-level security: only your authenticated account can read or write your rows.

3. Sign in with Apple

Caterpillar requires an account, created with Sign in with Apple, so your history survives losing or changing a phone. We receive an anonymous Apple identifier and, on first sign-in only, the name/email you approve (which may be Apple's private relay address). We never see your Apple ID password.

4. AI photo analysis

When you use AI Meal Scan or Menu Scan, your photo (compressed) is sent over an encrypted connection through our server function to our AI provider (Google's Gemini API) solely to generate the nutrition analysis, along with optional numeric portion measurements derived on-device from LiDAR (never raw depth data). The analysis result is returned to your device. We don't build advertising profiles from your photos.

5. Food search

Search queries are sent to food databases (our own curated database on-device, plus Open Food Facts, USDA and Australian government datasets via our server) to return results. If you use "Ask AI", the search text is processed by our AI provider.

6. Community and quality data

  • Food submissions and corrections you choose to make are sent to us for review (including an optional label photo) and may be shared with other users once approved.
  • When you scan a nutrition label and the reading is clear enough to be reliable, the product's name, brand and nutrition values are added to Caterpillar's shared verified-food database so other users benefit. Only product facts are shared — never anything identifying you.
  • The app sends anonymous, device-scoped reliability signals (e.g. a random install identifier and "this device received this food update") so we can tell whether features work — these are not tied to your identity or account.

7. Importing from other apps

If you import history from another tracker, you pick the file yourself and it is read entirely on your device — we don't browse your files and the file isn't uploaded anywhere. The meals, weigh-ins, water and activity it contains then become part of your own history (and your cloud backup, if signed in), exactly like data you logged in Caterpillar.

8. Notifications

Notifications are optional and off until you allow them. Reminders are scheduled and delivered on your device — their content (what you have or haven't logged) is never sent to a server to generate them.

9. What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad SDKs, no third-party analytics trackers.
  • No selling or renting personal information — ever.
  • No reading your Apple Health data off your device.

10. Retention and deletion

Cloud data is kept while your account exists. Delete your account in-app (Nest → Delete Account) and your request is actioned within 48 hours, permanently removing your cloud data. Deleting the app removes all local data immediately. You can also email us to exercise access or correction rights under the Australian Privacy Principles.

11. Children

Caterpillar is not directed at children and is not intended for users under 16.

12. Changes

If this policy changes materially we'll say so in the app before the change takes effect.
