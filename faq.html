How accurate is the AI Meal Scan?

It's an estimate — a careful one. The AI identifies each component, and on LiDAR-equipped iPhones real depth measurement sizes your portions rather than guessing. Packaged products are read off the nutrition panel, and calories are always recomputed on your phone from the macros, so a hallucinated calorie figure can't survive. A kitchen scale in frame is treated as the source of truth, and you can edit any item before logging.

Does it know the difference between cooked and dry pasta?

Yes, and this is where most trackers quietly go wrong. Pasta, rice, oats and lentils roughly triple in weight when cooked, so pricing a plate of dinner at dry-weight values overstates it by two or three times. Caterpillar treats food on a plate as cooked food, corrects the figure if the estimate came back at dry-weight values, and tells you it did.

Do I need an iPhone with LiDAR?

No. LiDAR (Pro models) makes portion sizing sharper, but every feature works on any supported iPhone — the AI falls back to careful visual estimation, blended with your own description if you give one.

Does coffee count toward hydration? What about alcohol?

Coffee counts, and so does tea — measured over a day they hydrate you about as well as water, which is not what folklore says. Alcohol depends entirely on strength: a beer is still net hydrating, wine is roughly break-even, and a neat spirit actually takes water back out of your dew drop. You can watch the drop fall when you log one. It seemed more useful to be honest than to be flattering.

Is Caterpillar free?

Free for core tracking — logging, scanning, hydration, streaks and the gallery. A Pro subscription (yearly, monthly or weekly) adds extras; pricing is shown in the app before you buy anything, and billing is handled by Apple. There are no ads at any tier.

Are there limits on the AI features?

There are fair-use ceilings on AI requests per hour and per day, so one person can't run the shared service flat out. They're set well above ordinary use — you'd have to be scanning continuously to notice. They apply to everyone, Pro included.

Are my meal photos uploaded?

A photo you scan is sent securely to our AI provider once, to generate the analysis, and the app then keeps a small compressed copy on your device for your timeline. If you submit a food correction, the photos you took as evidence travel with it so a human can check the numbers against the packaging.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

If you've signed in, your history, achievements, favourites and settings are backed up to your account — reinstall, sign in, and everything returns. Without an account, data lives only on your device and goes with the app. Either way you can export the lot to CSV first.

Where does my data live, and who can see it?

Synced data is stored with our backend provider (Supabase) and is readable only by your account, enforced at the database level. We don't run ads and we never sell personal data. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I sign in?

Sign in with Apple, with Google, or with an email address and password — whichever you prefer. An account exists so your history survives losing or changing a phone.

Does it work with Apple Health?

Yes, two-way. Caterpillar can read steps, active energy, exercise minutes and weight with your permission, and write your logged water and weigh-ins back to Health. You choose exactly which categories sync.

Can I bring my history from another app?

Yes. Export from your old tracker and import the file — it's read entirely on your device and never uploaded. Imported entries fill gaps in your history and never overwrite days you've already recorded.

Is it suitable if I've struggled with disordered eating?

Please talk to a professional first. Caterpillar is a general wellbeing tool, not a medical device, and calorie tracking isn't right for everyone. There's no shaming language and no punishment for going over, but that doesn't make it the right tool for every person.
