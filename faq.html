Questions, answered

If it's not here, email us — a human reads every message.

How accurate is the AI Meal Scan?

Honest answer: it's an estimate — a good one. For plated meals, the AI identifies each component and, on LiDAR-equipped iPhones, real depth measurement sizes your portions instead of guessing. For packaged products the app reads the nutrition label itself, and calories are always computed from the macros on-device. You can edit any item before logging, and a kitchen scale in frame is treated as the source of truth.

Do I need an iPhone with LiDAR?

No. LiDAR (iPhone Pro models) makes portion sizing sharper, but every feature works on any supported iPhone — the AI falls back to careful visual estimation.

Is Caterpillar free?

Caterpillar is free to use for core tracking. A Pro subscription is planned for launch with extras like meal-photo cloud backup and advanced goal tools — pricing will be announced in the app.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

If you've signed in with Apple, your history, achievements, favourites and settings are backed up to your account — reinstall, sign in, and everything returns. Without an account, data lives only on your device and is removed with the app.

Where does my data live, and who can see it?

Your synced data is stored with our backend provider (Supabase) and is readable only by your account — enforced at the database level. We don't run ads, and we never sell personal data. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Are my meal photos uploaded?

A photo you scan is sent securely to our AI provider once, to generate the analysis, then the app keeps a small copy on your device for your timeline. Cloud backup of meal photos is a planned Pro feature — free users' photos never leave the phone after analysis.

Does it work with Apple Health?

Yes — two-way. Caterpillar can read steps, active energy, exercise minutes and weight (with your permission), and write your logged water and weigh-ins back to Health. You choose exactly which categories sync.

Is the food database Australian?

Australian-first: AUSNUT and FSANZ data plus a curated local database of Aussie staples and brands, backed by Open Food Facts and USDA for global coverage. Barcode coverage of Woolworths, Coles and Aldi products is excellent.

What's the Butterfly Gallery?

Caterpillar's take on achievements: healthy habits earn puzzle pieces that gradually reveal collectible butterfly artwork across four rarity tiers. Some achievements are secret until you stumble into them. Completed art stays in your gallery for good.

Can it handle protein powders and meal-replacement shakes?

Yes — and properly. Log the serving and tell Caterpillar how much water you mixed it with: calories and macros stay per-serving while the mixing water counts toward hydration. Your pre-workout finally makes sense in a tracker.

Is this medical advice?

No. Caterpillar provides nutrition estimates and habit tools for general wellbeing. It's not a medical device and doesn't replace advice from a doctor or accredited dietitian — especially if you have a medical condition or history of disordered eating.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Nest → Delete Account. Your request is processed within 48 hours and removes your cloud data entirely. Made a mistake? Email us straight away and we'll cancel it.
