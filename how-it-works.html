From first scan to butterfly

Ninety seconds of setup, then the easiest logging you've used. Here's the whole loop.

1

Tell us where you're headed

A short onboarding builds your plan: current stats, goal weight (lose, maintain or gain — your call), activity level, and how much of your exercise you want to "earn back" as food. Caterpillar computes an honest daily calorie and macro target using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, with healthy floors so a crash diet is never on the menu.

2

Log the way that suits the moment

Cooked a meal? AI Meal Scan photographs the plate; LiDAR (on supported iPhones) measures real portion volume so 150 g of pasta isn't logged as 300 g. Packaged food? The scanner reads the label itself — macros come from the panel, not a guess. Groceries? Barcode scan, tuned for Australian shelves. Eating out? Menu Scan reads the menu and estimates your dish. And search understands Aussie shorthand — "parmi", "maccas", "weet-bix" all just work.

3

Watch your garden respond

Your caterpillar eats when you log, drinks when you hydrate, and your dew drop fills through the day — each drink coloured and counted at its real hydration value (a beer is not a glass of water, sorry). Macros, steps, active energy and streaks all live on one Garden screen.

4

Earn your gallery

Consistency unlocks puzzle pieces; puzzle pieces reveal collectible butterfly artwork across Wood, Silver, Gold and Diamond tiers — plus secret achievements you'll have to discover. It's the kind of streak pressure that feels like play instead of guilt.

5

Keep it forever

Sign in with Apple and everything — history, streaks, gallery, favourites, settings — is backed up to your account. New phone or reinstall, one sign-in brings it all home. Change your mind? Delete your account from inside the app and it's gone within 48 hours.
